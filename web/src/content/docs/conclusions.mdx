The spec asks every member for an individual sheet with three parts:

1. **A contribution** linked to a commit, review or test.
2. **An annotated trace** they can reproduce (PC, bytes, fields, control signals, buffers, state before and after).
3. **A conclusion** about a decision or a fix of their own.

The traces in [Tests and traces](/tests/#traces-r-i-j) show the format; each member annotates one of their own.
